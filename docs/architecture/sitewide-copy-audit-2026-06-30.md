## Standard

Each page was judged as a visitor-facing sales or trust surface, not as an internal architecture document.

- **P — Pass:** sounds human, serves the reader, and has a clear next step appropriate to the page.
- **F — Fail:** contains template language, internal web-design commentary, unnecessary jargon, weak sequencing, or a call to action that does not feel like the natural next move.

The preferred sequence is:

1. Name the reader's problem in recognizable language.
2. Show how Leverage AI solves it.
3. Support the claim with proof, specificity, or research.
4. Help the reader picture the improved reality.
5. Offer a low-friction next step.
