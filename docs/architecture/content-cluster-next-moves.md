## Recommended Sequence

1. Deepen the local demand and response cluster first.
2. Add the GBP and diagnostic-audit clusters next because they connect directly to pricing and contact.
3. Expand proof with one or two more case studies.
4. Only then widen the industry cluster aggressively.

## Working Rule

Every new page should do three things:

1. link back to at least one service page
2. reinforce an existing proof or report surface
3. move the reader toward pricing, contact, or the diagnostic audit
