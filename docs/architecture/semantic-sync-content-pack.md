## What To Reuse From The Report

Keep:

- the shift from keyword-level thinking to entity-level trust
- the Google Business Profile plus website dual-anchor framing
- the relevance, proximity, and prominence explanation
- the structured-data and FAQ guidance
- the emphasis on reviews, photos, and answer-first writing

Do not carry over as-is:

- heavy jargon without translation
- national-template phrasing that ignores Oregon market context
- abstract SEO language that does not map back to booked local demand

## Tone Conversion Rule

Every page derived from this report should translate strategy language into business-owner language.

Preferred:

- "keep your public signals aligned"
- "make your service and location pages support each other"
- "reduce guesswork for buyers, Google, and AI search"

Avoid leading with:

- "semantic synchronization" without explanation
- generic SEO abstractions with no local-business application
