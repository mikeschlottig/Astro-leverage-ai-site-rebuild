Decisions needed

  1. D1 — Retainer floor. Today’s top recurring price is $400–$800/mo (pricing.astro:38). The recommendation publishes $1,500–$10,000. That is a business call, not a copy edit. Replace the old tier, or keep it as a fourth “Maintenance” rung for existing clients?

  3. D3 — Specialist tier. It sells legal and real-estate GEO, and neither vertical page nor proof exists. Recommend publishing Foundation and Growth now and showing Specialist as “by application” until a vertical page is live.
  4. D4 — Research location. Give the path to the file holding the briefs, the eight post outlines and the cited statistics. Every number on a public page needs a source line.
  5. D5 — Social parent. Recommend local-seo (its work is profile and NAP consistency). Alternative: leave it under the new AI Search hub.
  6. D6 — Software development. Recommend rename in place to “Automation and AI Systems” (stays a hub, last in order). Alternative: fold under the intake hub.
  7. D7 — workers.dev origin. It serves a full duplicate with no noindex. Recommend a non-canonical-host X-Robots-Tag, not disabling the host (PUBLIC_SITE_ORIGIN is used at worker/contact.ts:149).
