## Tone Translation Rule

Both reports need translation before they become public site assets.

Keep:

- the local-trust logic
- the cross-platform entity logic
- the audit-first rationale
- the distinction between traffic and trusted visibility

Do not keep as-is:

- generic "future of SEO" phrasing
- inflated benchmark language without local-business translation
- startup or SaaS framing that does not map to service businesses clearly

## Audit Pitch Rule

The strongest conversion-facing phrasing from these reports is:

- GBP optimization and a strong website matter, but they are only part of the equation.
- The real advantage is becoming a trusted and authoritative entity across platforms.
- This is not just an SEO audit.
- It is a comprehensive entity evaluation from the perspective of search engines, LLMs, and potential customers.
- The audit creates the understanding first.
- Then comes the blueprint, the playbook, and the execution.
