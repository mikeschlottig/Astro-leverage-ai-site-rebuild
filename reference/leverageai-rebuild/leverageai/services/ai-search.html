The Method

How AI search visibility is built.

Audit
Citation baseline and competitive gap
Before building anything, I map your current AI citation presence across all major platforms, benchmark against your direct competitors, and identify exactly where the gaps are and why they exist. No guesswork — all evidence.
Entity
Entity clarity and disambiguation
AI systems cite brands they can confidently identify. I ensure your brand entity is unambiguously defined across the web — your name, location, services, and relationships are consistent everywhere AI systems look. Structured data, NAP consistency, Wikipedia signals, knowledge graph alignment.
Schema
JSON-LD schema implementation
Schema.org markup is the language AI systems use to understand content structure. I implement comprehensive structured data: Organization, LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ, Person, BreadcrumbList — the complete taxonomy that makes your content machine-readable.
Content
Architecture engineered for LLM comprehension
LLMs extract information differently than Google's crawler. Content architecture for AI search means entity-dense writing, clear semantic structure, Q&A patterns that match how AI retrieves answers, and authority signals that make your brand a trustworthy source to cite.
Monitor
Citation velocity tracking and adaptation
AI search is a moving target. Monthly tracking across platforms, competitor monitoring, and strategy adaptation as the AI landscape shifts. You see exactly where your brand appears, how often, and what's driving changes — in plain language, not jargon.
