Design Philosophy

Research drives decisions. Craft makes them last.

Most design agencies start with trends and work backward to justify them. I start with your market, your audience, and the specific conversion outcome you need — then build the visual system that serves those constraints.

Good design isn't decoration. It's the structure that makes information trustworthy, navigation obvious, and the next action clear. Every pixel should earn its place.

What this isn't
Template customization. Trend-chasing. "Here are three options, pick one." I deliver a single, well-considered direction — the right one for your brand — with the reasoning behind every choice.
Research
Market and competitor audit first
I map your competitive landscape visually before making a single design decision. What are your competitors communicating? What's the gap? What would make you unmistakably different?
Constraint
Design for your real users
Who actually buys from you, on what device, in what context? A mobile-first local service business has different design constraints than a B2B SaaS conversion page. Both require precision; neither requires the same solution.
Systems
Tokens, not one-offs
I build design systems, not pages. Every color, spacing value, and type style exists in a documented system that scales — whether you're adding a new page or a new product line next year.
Ownership
You own everything
Figma source files, exported assets, design documentation. No proprietary tools, no lock-in. If you want to take the work elsewhere, it's completely portable.
Scope

Every layer of the visual system.

Design strategy isn't a logo. It's the complete visual language — every color, every typeface, every spacing decision — working as a unified system.

Brand Identity
Logo system, mark variants, color palette, typography selection. The foundation every other design decision builds on.
UI/UX Design
Key page layouts designed for conversion: homepage, service pages, landing pages, contact. High-fidelity, responsive, production-ready.
Design System
Documented component library with color tokens, spacing scale, typography styles, and interaction states. Figma source included.
Accessibility
WCAG AA compliance built in from the start. Contrast ratios, focus states, semantic structure — not an afterthought.
Responsive Design
Designed for every screen from the start. Mobile, tablet, desktop — fluid layouts that don't break when viewed on a phone.
Implementation Support
I can build it, not just design it. Full HTML/CSS/JS implementation available, or handoff specs for your development team.
