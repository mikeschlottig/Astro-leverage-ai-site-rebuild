The Approach

Dashboards built around decisions, not displays.

Most analytics implementations start with the tool and work backward to the question. I start with the decision you need to make — what to build next, where to invest, whether the current strategy is working — and build the data architecture that answers it.

That means custom dashboards, not GA4 reports. It means tracking the metrics that predict growth, not the ones that look impressive in a quarterly review.

Web traffic is a vanity number in 2026. What matters is citation velocity, GBP conversion rate, and the signals that tell you where high-intent buyers are finding you — before they call.

What Gets Tracked

The metrics that predict growth.

Not every metric deserves a dashboard widget. These are the signals I track because they actually correlate with business outcomes — phone calls, form fills, and closed deals.

AI Citation Frequency
How often your brand appears in AI-generated answers across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude. Tracked weekly, reported monthly.
Example: 47 citations/week → 189/week in 90 days
Organic Impression Growth
Search Console impressions and click-through rate, segmented by branded vs. non-branded and by intent category.
Leading indicator: impressions precede clicks by 3–6 weeks
GBP Conversion Rate
Google Business Profile views to calls and direction requests. The clearest signal of local buyer intent and the most undertracked metric in local business.
Industry avg: 1–3% · Optimized: 6–12%
Entity Authority Score
A composite signal measuring how confidently AI systems can identify and recommend your brand based on structured data, consistency, and citation density.
Proprietary scoring framework — updated monthly
Citation Velocity Trend
Not just how many citations — how fast the number is growing. Velocity is the signal that separates a strategy that's working from one that's plateaued.
Target: sustained 15–25% monthly growth rate
Competitive Gap Index
Your citation presence vs. direct competitors, indexed to show where you're winning, where you're tied, and where the highest-value opportunity sits.
Updated quarterly — drives strategy prioritization
