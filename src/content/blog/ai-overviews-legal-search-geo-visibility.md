## From 12% to 77% in two years

Industry research reports that in 2024 only 12% of legal consumer discovery queries involved AI answer citations. By 2026, more than 77% of all legal search queries trigger Google AI Overviews, the highest rate of any industry or professional services vertical it compared.

At the same time, people ask conversational questions directly in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Microsoft Copilot. A search like "best personal injury lawyer" in ChatGPT returns specific named firms, not a list of links.

## The decoupling: ranking first, cited nowhere

Empirical testing in 2026 shows that a law firm page can rank first in the traditional results for a valuable keyword and still be left out of the AI Overview. The reverse also happens: an entity-optimized, well-structured page can be cited by an AI Overview and recommended by ChatGPT without ranking on the first page.

That breaks the assumption behind most legal SEO reporting. Agencies that still lead with third-party Domain Authority scores, exact-match keyword density and ranking tables are reporting on a layer that no longer decides who gets named.

## Why AI referrals convert

The Ruler Analytics study of more than 110 million sessions puts the average legal conversion rate at 7.9%. By channel: referral 8.8%, AI referral 8.4%, paid search 4.3% to 5.48%, direct 4.2%, organic search 3.0% to 4.3%, email 2.5%, paid social 2.2%, organic social 2.1%.

Separate specialist agency studies of high-intent legal queries report that visitors arriving from AI answer engines convert at up to 23 times the rate of top-of-funnel informational organic visitors. The mechanism is plain: the assistant has already heard the person's legal issue and recommended a specific firm before the click. The visitor arrives pre-qualified.

## What GEO for a law firm actually involves

Generative engine optimization, GEO, is the work of making a firm easy for assistants to find, understand and cite. The sequence leading firms use:

## Share of voice: the new KPI

Share of voice here means the proportion of relevant questions, across the assistants that matter, in which your firm is named or cited. It moves differently from rankings. Track it monthly alongside calls and booked consultations, and read it as a trend rather than a score. Results vary by assistant and change over time, so no one can honestly guarantee a citation.

## What this means for a smaller firm

The research forecasts that AI Overviews and conversational assistants may reach 80% to 85% of legal queries between 2028 and 2031, and that informational blog traffic will shrink while AI referral clicks carry more of the case value. That forecast is an inference, not a measurement.

What is measured today is enough to act on: the answer layer is already in front of most legal searches, and the firms it names are the ones with consistent entity data and clear, structured answers.
