> **In short:** Automation and AI systems from Leverage AI LLC in Grants Pass, Oregon are the engine behind fast lead response and clear reporting: forms that write to a database and alert your team, dashboards, custom applications and AI features with written rules. Each project starts with the smallest useful version and ships with tests, error handling and documentation.

## What are automation and AI systems for a small business?

They are programs built for one business's specific process, such as intake, scheduling, quoting or reporting. They fit your workflow instead of forcing your workflow to fit a product.

For service businesses in Southern Oregon this usually means connecting what already exists: the website form, the customer list, the calendar and the invoicing tool. The most common result is the lead capture and AI follow-up service, which runs on this work.

## What kinds of projects do you take on?

The projects fall into three groups. Applications include web and mobile apps and Rails or Python systems. Data work includes crawlers, extraction, dashboards and data cleanup. AI work includes implementation and governance: what the AI may do, who approves it, and what gets logged.

## How are projects scoped and delivered?

We write the smallest useful version first, agree on it, build it, and then extend. That keeps cost and risk visible at each step.

Delivery includes tests, error handling, documentation and a handoff session so your team knows how it works and who to call.

## Who owns the work?

You do. When the contract period ends, the business owns the code and the digital assets built for it. A system you cannot take with you is a rental, and we do not sell rentals.
