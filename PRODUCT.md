# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary users are owners and operators of Oregon service businesses who need more qualified local demand, stronger trust signals, and faster lead response without getting trapped in generic agency retainers.

They are usually evaluating the site in a practical buying context: comparing providers, checking whether the work feels credible, deciding whether the operator understands their market, and looking for proof that the system can turn attention into calls, forms, and booked jobs.

Secondary users include technical or marketing collaborators, referral partners, and AI/crawler systems that need a clean, trustworthy understanding of the business, its services, and its proof.

## Product Purpose

Leverage AI exists to present a systems-first, result-driven service business growth model: local visibility, custom website architecture, lead capture, immediate response, and measurement working together instead of as disconnected tactics.

The site should function as both a high-conviction brand surface and a structured authority engine. It needs to:

- communicate real value fast
- prove local market understanding
- connect every route through deliberate linking and clustering
- turn long-form branded assets into domain authority builders
- support app and Worker infrastructure without making the brand feel like a software dashboard

Success looks like a site where every major page has a job, every content cluster reinforces a service outcome, and visitors leave with a clear sense that this is a grounded, premium operator who builds systems that produce real business results.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, premium, outcome-led.

The voice should feel sharp, direct, and informed by real operating experience. It should sound like a capable founder-operator who understands leverage, local business reality, and the economics of delayed response. It should avoid inflated posture, vague futurism, or generic AI-marketing language.

Emotionally, the site should evoke confidence, clarity, strategic tension, and forward motion.

## Anti-references

- Generic "AI slop" marketing sites full of buzzwords, fake metrics, and decorative claims.
- SaaS-looking pricing grids and toy-card layouts that make the business feel cheaper than it is.
- Agency sites that lead with jargon, empty trend language, or dashboards detached from real outcomes.
- Fake testimonials, fake proof, or synthetic authority signals presented as real results.
- Directionless content sprawl where pages are not systematically linked through hierarchy and clusters.

## Design Principles

- Result First: lead with business outcomes, not technical vocabulary.
- Systematic Linking: every page must belong to a hierarchy, a cluster, and a deliberate internal-link graph.
- Real Proof Over Vanity: prioritize factual case studies, grounded research, and clearly labeled illustrative examples.
- Premium Through Restraint And Precision: the surface should feel intentional and elevated, not loud, cluttered, or trend-chasing.
- Brand Surface, App Support: the public experience is a brand system first; Workers, forms, and app logic exist to support that experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The baseline is WCAG AA with reduced motion support across the site.

The experience should preserve contrast, readable long-form typography, clear keyboard focus, and meaningful structure in the initial HTML. Motion should add atmosphere without hiding content or making the experience harder to follow.
